### B2B — Commercial & Industrial

#### Business Intelligence
Market size, buyer segments and purchase trends so you invest where commercial buyers already spend.

1. Which industries and regions do you target?
2. What decision will this inform: market entry, pricing, new product or new territory?
3. Who are your current top customers, and what do they buy?
4. What data do you already have (CRM records, sales history, distributor reports)?
5. What output do you prefer: one-time report, Power BI dashboard or monthly briefing?

#### Competitor Intelligence
Who wins your target accounts, at what price, and why — with a SWOT per competitor.

1. Which competitors do you already know about?
2. Where are you losing deals: price, technical fit, lead time or relationships?
3. Can you share lost-deal notes or competitor price points?
4. Do you need a one-time landscape or a detailed SWOT for each competitor?

#### Sales Pipeline Management
I drive and lead your sales team: pipeline stages, activity targets, reviews and forecasting.

1. How many people are on your sales team, and what are their roles?
2. Which CRM do you use, and how current is it?
3. What is your current pipeline value, and what stages do you track?
4. How long is your typical sales cycle?
5. Will I have authority to set activity targets and run weekly reviews?

#### Channel Partner Management
Distributor, reseller and system integrator programs that produce orders, not just agreements.

1. Which partners do you have now, and in which regions?
2. Are partner or target agreements in place?
3. What is the main problem: channel conflict, inactive partners, pricing or coverage gaps?
4. Do you want to recruit new partners, grow existing ones, or both?

#### Competitor Activity & Strategy Monitoring
Ongoing watch on competitors' pricing, launches, partnerships and key accounts.

1. Which competitors should be watched?
2. What should be tracked: pricing, product launches, partnerships, key hires or account wins?
3. How often do you want updates: weekly or monthly?

#### Sales Target Achievement
Close the gap between target and actual with KPI discipline, account focus and DSO control.

1. What is the target (revenue, volume or margin), and for what period?
2. How did the last period perform against target?
3. What do you see as the reasons for the gap?
4. Which products or accounts have the most upside?
5. Which KPIs do you track today?

#### Sector / Vertical Focused
Sales built around one industry: Oil & Gas, petrochemical, power, EPC, manufacturing, healthcare or IT.

1. Which sector do you want to grow in?
2. What references or installed base do you already have there?
3. Which vendor approvals do you hold (e.g. owner or EPC vendor lists)?
4. Which target accounts or EPCs matter most?

### B2G — Government & Defense
