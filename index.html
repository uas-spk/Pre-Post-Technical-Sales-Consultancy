How is the work priced?

USD 75 per hour is the base rate for B2B and B2G work. Packages are quoted after a short assessment of your scenario. Defense and NATO RFPs are USD 200 per hour, with a custom quote after assessment.

How do I get a quote?

Pick the B2B or B2G scenario that fits, click Request a quote, and answer the questions in the email. I assess your inputs and reply with scope, timeline and price.

Send it anyway. Describe what you need, your market and your timeline, and I will scope it.

Yes. Under Sales Pipeline Management or Capture & Pipeline Management I set activity targets, run pipeline reviews and forecast with your team.

Yes. I help with registrations, contract-vehicle qualification, teaming partners and the first bids, so you reach government buyers in compliance.

USA, Canada, Saudi Arabia / GCC and Pakistan, for commercial, industrial and government buyers.

Is my information kept confidential?
