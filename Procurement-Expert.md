## Pre-Bid Risk Mitigation Framework

Every risk below is closed **before bid submission**, not discovered after award.

| # | Stage | Risks Mitigated | Output |
|:---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | **RFP Intake & Decomposition** | Missed mandatory requirements, misread rated criteria, ambiguous specifications | Requirements compliance matrix; clarification questions issued within the RFP Q&A window |
| 2 | **Technical Compliance & Standards** | Non-compliant products, missing certifications (UL, CSA, IEC, IEEE, NEMA, CE, FCC, ISO, MIL-STD, IP ratings) | Spec-for-spec compliance confirmed with the OEM; certificates on file |
| 3 | **OEM & Vendor Authorization** | Unauthorized resale, void warranties, unsupported products | OEM authorization letters, reseller status, warranty and support confirmation |
| 4 | **Supply Chain & Lead Time** | Lead times exceeding the delivery date, single-source dependency, country-of-origin and trade-agreement issues | Confirmed lead times, alternate sources, origin documentation |
| 5 | **Commercial & Pricing** | Quote validity shorter than bid validity, currency and freight exposure, hidden duties | Comparative statement of vendor quotes; Incoterms, freight and duties built into the price |
| 6 | **Site Readiness & Installation** | Insufficient power or cooling capacity, site access, rigging and installation constraints | Site survey findings and installation prerequisites stated in the bid |
| 7 | **Delivery, Testing & Commissioning** | Failed acceptance, untested equipment, missing documentation | FAT / SAT plan, commissioning procedure, as-built documents and training plan |
| 8 | **Contractual & Legal** | Liquidated damages, warranty flow-down gaps, insurance and security-clearance requirements | Back-to-back terms with vendors and subcontractors |
| 9 | **Bid / No-Bid Decision** | Bidding on opportunities that cannot be won or delivered | Pwin, competition assessment and Bid / No-Bid recommendation |
